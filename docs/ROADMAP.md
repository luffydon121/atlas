5. Patent, procurement and construction permit connectors.

1. Free-form widget workspace with several saved layouts.
2. Object detection on user-supplied high-resolution imagery.
3. Multi-asset factor backtests with point-in-time universes.
4. DuckDB and Parquet for large historical sets.
